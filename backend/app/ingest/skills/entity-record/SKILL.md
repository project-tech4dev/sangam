# The record we keep for one organisation

One organisation, one record. A person opens it to answer a specific question
and leaves when they have the answer, so the order is the order the questions
get asked — not the order the research happened in.

The published file at `docs/entities/<slug>.md` is generated by
`app/ingest/brief.py` from the concept registry. This skill is the
specification that generation satisfies, and it is what a hand-written
on-screen summary of one organisation follows too. A section named here that
has no concept behind it will not appear in the generated file — that is a
gap to report, not a line to invent.

## Order

**For an organisation that receives money**, the first question ends the
conversation if the answer is wrong, so it goes first:

1. **Identity** — name as the strongest source writes it, registration type,
   legal form, when it was registered, where it is registered.
2. **Statutory standing** — 12A, 80G, CSR-1 and its registration number, PAN,
   DARPAN with blacklist status, FCRA kept separate. Each with its number and
   whether it is current. A cancelled or suspended registration appears here,
   not in a footnote, with its appeal status beside it.
3. **Money** — income, programme spend, non-programme spend and foreign
   contribution, by year, with whether the accounts were audited.
4. **Where it works** — state and district, with the registered address shown
   as what it is rather than as a separate fact.
5. **What it does** — programmes, then reach and outcomes.
6. **Who funds it already** — named funders with year and amount. These are the
   funders' `partner` lines naming this organisation — `funder_dossier` with
   `entity_kind='org'` lists them under grants received — or its `partner`
   findings when not yet promoted. Write "not recorded" rather than omitting
   the heading.
7. **Governance and capacity** — board, permanent staff, institutional
   policies.
8. **Contacts**, then **source documents**.

**For a funder**, the first question is whether there is a door:

1. **Identity** — name, the exact legal entity that receives applications, its
   parent, type, whether it is Section 135 bound.
2. **How to approach it** — intake route, whether it was live when checked and
   when that was, grant size, duration, co-funding share, whether this is a
   live call or a historical disclosure.
3. **Who it will fund** — eligibility with the statutory restatement kept
   separate from the funder's own bar, hard disqualifiers, and the document
   checklist verbatim.
4. **CSR position by year**, then **project and sector spend**.
5. **Where the work ran**, **implementing partners**, **programmes**,
   **reach and outcomes**.
6. **Open calls**, **contacts**, **source documents**.

## Every line

A fact carries four things and loses none of them:

- the **value**, with its unit written out — amounts in the source's own words
  beside the figure, never a bare number
- **evidenced** or **claimed**, shown, never silently upgraded
- the **source URL**, inline, not collected into a footnote
- the **access state** when it is not a plain readable page — `scanned`,
  `archived` or `soft-404`, because an absence read off a scanned PDF is not
  an absence

A year-bearing fact shows its year. A fact that is inferred rather than
stated says so.

## Missing values

**Never leave a blank and never drop a heading.** An empty cell is ambiguous
between "we did not look", "we looked and the source was silent" and "the
source says it does not exist", and those three carry completely different
next actions. Write which one it is:

- `not recorded` — we did not look, or the research stopped first
- `not stated` — we read the source and it is silent, with the URL we read
- `stated as absent` — a source says it does not exist
- `found, not storable` — the source published it and no column exists for
  it. Writing "not stated" over one of these misrepresents the source, and it
  is the only one of the four that is our gap rather than theirs.

The "Looked for, not found" section carries the second and third. It is not
optional, and "not recorded is not the same as not existing" stays on it.

## Lines that are computed, not written

An eligibility line — which statutory gate blocks CSR money today — is
**derived from the evidenced findings**, never written as an opinion. It names
the blocking gate ("CSR-1 missing", "80G and CSR-1 missing", "12A cancelled,
under appeal") rather than saying "ineligible", and it counts only evidenced
values. A claimed 12A does not clear a gate.

Never score, rank or rate. A funder's own criteria decide fit, and a rating
invented here is a judgement wearing a citation.

## The header

Name, the date researched, the run status, and the one-paragraph summary
before any section. If the research stopped early, the reason goes in the
header where it cannot be missed — a partial record read as a complete one is
the failure this prevents.

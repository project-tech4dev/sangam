You group news items and social posts into STORIES. A story is one real-world event, announcement, report, court ruling,
programme activity or post: the same thing reported by several outlets, syndicated copies, an op-ed or letter reacting
to that same specific event, or the same post shared on Instagram, LinkedIn, X and so on. Different events about the
same broad topic (two different FCRA cancellations, two different CSR reports, a rally on one day and a statement by a
different party on another day) are DIFFERENT stories. Keys starting with "s" are stories already stored; never put two
of them in one group. Every key must appear in exactly one group; a group of one is fine.

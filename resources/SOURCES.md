# Data sources

Where Sangam's data comes from.

## NGO

| # | Resource | Official site | Link | What you can collect | How to reach the data on the official site |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | NGO Darpan | NGO Darpan – NITI Aayog | https://ngodarpan.gov.in/ | NGO name, registration details, registering act, office bearers, contact email and phone, website, sectors and areas of operation | NGO Darpan → Search NGO → filter by state, sector or name → open the NGO → view its details. |
| 2 | NGO official websites | Each NGO's own website (listed on its NGO Darpan profile) | Varies per NGO | Programmes, history, team and contacts, annual report summaries, impact and beneficiary numbers, funders and partners, financials, news and updates | Open the NGO's website → About Us (history) → Programmes / Our Work → Team → Annual Reports / Publications → Contact Us. |
| 3 | Give | Give | https://give.do/ | NGO profiles, causes, programmes, verified nonprofit information | Give → search the NGO → open its profile → Overview → Programs → People → About Us → Field Visit. |

## CSR / Funder

| # | Resource | Official site | Link | What you can collect | How to reach the data on the official site |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | MCA CSR Data | MCA Corporate Data Management – CSR Data | — | Company name, FY, CSR expenditure, state, district, CSR development sector, PSU/non-PSU, implementing agency, project/programme information, expenditure trends | MCA CDM → CSR Data → Financial Year → PSU/Non-PSU → CSR State → CSR District → CSR Development Sector → Export Data. The current portal provides filters for FY, state, district and development sector and an Export Data option. |
| 2 | CSR / funder official websites | Each company's or foundation's own website | Varies per funder | CSR policy, CSR committee members, annual action plan, focus areas, flagship programmes, implementing partners and NGOs funded, geographies, grant or application process, eligibility, contacts, CSR section of the annual report, impact reports | Open the company or foundation website → Sustainability / CSR / Foundation → CSR Policy → Annual Action Plan → Programmes / Projects → Partners → Investors → Annual Reports (CSR annexure) → Contact Us. |
| 3 | Give | Give | https://give.do/ | Funder type (e.g. corporate foundation), grant value by financial year, cause-level grant distribution, CSR policy link, policy themes, geographical coverage, programs, leadership team, contact, website | Give → search the funder → open its profile → Grant Value → Cause Area → CSR Policy → Policy Themes → Geographical Coverage → Programs → Leadership Team → Contact. |

**Example export:** [`Dynamic_CSR_Report_2026-09-28.csv`](Dynamic_CSR_Report_2026-09-28.csv), exported from MCA CSR Data on 2026-09-28: 5,450 rows for Assam, FY 2014-15 to FY 2024-25. Columns: Company Name, Financial Year, PSU/Non-PSU, CSR State, CSR District, CSR Development Sector, CSR Sub Development Sector, Project Amount Spent (In INR Cr.).
